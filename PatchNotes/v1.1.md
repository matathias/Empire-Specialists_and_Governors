# Empire Refactored: Specialists & Governors v1.1

## 1.1.2 - Visitors Update
- Date: 2026-07-26
- Type: Patch
- Authors: Matathias

A small patch to prevent potential issues or exploits when used alongside mods like Hospitality.

### Changes
- Bugfix: if a specialist is present on the player's map (e.g. a Hospitality visit), then recalling the specialist would have the potential for buggy behavior

## 1.1.1 - Various Fixes
- Date: 2026-07-13
- Type: Patch
- Authors: Matathias

Various fixes and minor changes.

### Changes
- Bugfix: the role-assignment menu for caravans can strand pawns in the world void if the role they would be assigned to is already at its cap
- Bugfix: a manual defense battle could unintentionally roll defeat death chances against each specialist
- Bugfix: if an empire settlement is removed, the specialists within would be lost to the void
- Bugfix: a cache invalidation issue caused specialist skill increases to not actually alter the bonuses they supply until after the game is saved and loaded
- When recalling specialists from an orbital settlement, deliver them to the player by drop pod instead of forming a caravan in space
- Revives the Professional Army and Garrison Doctrine traits that fell by the wayside during the 0.3 role rework. Flesh out Meritocratic
- Add mod version to the mod settings

- Authors: Matathias

Updates the mod to handle the new daily resource cadence introduced by Empire Refactored v1.6.
