- Date: 2026-06-17
- Type: Minor
- Authors: Matathias

Numerous fixes, updates, and a minor feature.

- Governors, Specialists, and Residents can now be easily sent directly by transport pod
- Bugfix: pawns that were recalled from a settlement were incorrectly removed from the world pawn pool, resulting in them disappearing into the void on the next caravan tick
