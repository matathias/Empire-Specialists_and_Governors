# Empire Refactored: Specialists & Governors v1.2

## 1.2.0 - RimWorld 1.7 Update
- Date: 2026-11-08
- Type: Major
- Authors: Matathias

Update for Empire and RimWorld 1.7.

### Changes
- Various code and file changes/cleanup/modernization
- Every load, mark all specialists as ForceKeep so vanilla doesn't attempt to abscond with them (plus additional hardening to ensure specialists are always KeepForever)
- Bugfix: assigning specialists from a caravan could trap goods in the settlement, or stand a caravan with only animals
- Block quest pawns and borrowed pawns from being assignable as specialists
- Specialists that are away from the settlement for whatever reason don't get pulled into settlement defense
- Death-chance tooltip shows the Garrison Doctrine Commander reduction
