# Empire Refactored: Specialists & Governors

This mod allows you to send your pawns to Empire settlements, where they can passively boost those settlements as residents, specialists, or governors. Pawns that are assigned to settlements are removed from your control and added to the Empire faction — but never fear, for you can retrieve them at any time.

The bonuses a pawn provides depend on the role they are assigned.

* **Governors** provide a multiplicative resource bonus based on their skills and chosen resource focus.
* **Specialists** provide a flat additive resource bonus based on their skills — or, alternatively, a bonus to the settlement's military defenses.
* **Residents** provide an additional 1 worker per 5 residents or so. (This role is meant to be a way for you to 'keep' pawns around without having to worry about them).

Assigning pawns to a settlement is as simple as sending them via a caravan.

This assignment isn't completely free, however. Specialists and Governors cost silver to upkeep, and whenever a settlement loses a battle, there is a small chance that assigned pawns will die.

If you play with Routes & Resources, then specialists and governors will also have a food and medicine need. Be sure to keep them happy and well-fed!
